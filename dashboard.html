Standardised classifications. Postings are classified two ways: by occupation at the 4-digit ISCO-08 unit-group (ISCO-4) level, and by employer sector at the 2-digit ISIC Rev.4 division (ISIC-2) level. The ISCO-4 Occupations and Employer Sectors (ISIC-2) sub-tabs each carry a ranked chart and a full code-labelled listing; By Region breaks occupational demand down by state. All header filters apply.
